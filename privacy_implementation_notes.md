# Privacy Implementation Notes

This technical review uses the California Attorney General’s CCPA overview and Global Privacy Control guidance, plus the California Privacy Protection Agency FAQ. These sources explain that covered businesses must honor valid opt-out preference signals, provide a clear opt-out method when required, support consumer rights requests, and meet response-time obligations. They do not substitute for legal advice or a business-specific applicability assessment.

| Technical area | Official guidance relevant to implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Opt-out of sale/sharing | The CCPA overview describes the right to opt out of sale or sharing and explains that covered businesses must honor user-enabled Global Privacy Control signals. |
| Privacy requests | The CPPA FAQ discusses rights to know, delete, correct, opt out, limit sensitive-personal-information use, and non-discrimination. |
| Request timing | The CPPA FAQ describes receipt confirmation within 10 business days and substantive response generally within 45 calendar days for requests to know, delete, or correct; opt-out/limit requests have a maximum 15-business-day implementation timeframe. |
